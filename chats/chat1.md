## User

Make UI mockups for Concept: A karaoke bar & lounge site that feels like walking into Vegas at night — energetic, a little glamorous, built around music and nightlife, but still easy to navigate on mobile (most visitors will be checking hours/location on their phone before heading out).

Sections (single-page, scroll-based):

Hero — full-bleed, moody bar/stage photo or gradient, big bold logo lockup, tagline, two CTAs: "Book a Table" / "See Tonight's Lineup"
About — short story of the club, vibe, what makes it different
Karaoke — how it works (private rooms? open stage? song catalog?), schedule (which nights), booking info
Drinks/Menu — cocktails, beer, bottle service — teaser cards linking to a full menu (PDF or page)
Gallery — photos/video of the space, past nights
Events/Specials — happy hour, ladies' night, themed nights, etc.
Location & Hours — map embed, address, opening hours, age policy (21+/18+ depending on Bhutan's laws)
Contact/Reserve — phone, socials, reservation form or WhatsApp link

Visual direction (pending your logo/colors):

Dark base (near-black or deep maroon/purple) so neon/gold accents pop
One accent color pulled from your logo for CTAs and highlights
Bold display font for headlines (something with Vegas/marquee energy), clean sans-serif for body text
Subtle motion: glow/pulse on buttons, smooth scroll reveals — nothing that tanks mobile performance

Tech approach: Since it's for real customers to check hours, book, and browse the menu, I'd build it as a proper responsive site (Next.js, matching your usual stack) rather than a single static page — easier to add an admin-editable events/menu later if you want.
